Which Dinner Lady Is The Witch?

Today's dinner: roast potatoes, peas and green custard

How to play: one of the four dinner ladies is secretly a witch. You get 5 peeks. Peek in a pocket, a pot or at some shoes to find a clue. Witchy clues are purple. Careful: normal dinner ladies can have one weird thing about them too. The witch always has two. When you're sure, press Accuse!
